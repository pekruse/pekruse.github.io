My Story

Personal statement

One question that stuck with me in the electronics lab was why an amplifier could produce a clean signal on the oscilloscope and then clip when I connected a speaker. I like having a prediction, a measurement, and a reason to look again.

I study electrical and computer engineering at the University at Buffalo, alongside a minor in law. I’m fascinated by the intersection of AI and hardware, especially how a device’s sensors, computing power, and energy budget shape what it can do. That curiosity also draws me to photonic computing and the possibility of using light to process information.

I’m looking for industry experience where I can contribute at the bench, learn from engineers who have shipped hardware, and see how a design holds up in someone else’s hands.
