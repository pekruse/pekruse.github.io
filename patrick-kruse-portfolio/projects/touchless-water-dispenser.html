Touchless Water Dispenser

I want a dispenser that can fill bottles of different shapes and stop before they overflow. My idea is to listen to the bottle as it fills.

Listening to the fill

A motion sensor would trigger dispensing while a microphone records the sound. I’d filter the signal and look for changes in resonance as the air space above the water shrinks.

First test

I’d record bottles of different shapes and materials at known fill levels. The question is whether the sound gives enough warning to stop the flow reliably, even with splashing and background noise.
