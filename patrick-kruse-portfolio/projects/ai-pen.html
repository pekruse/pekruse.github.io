Tracking the pen tip

An inertial measurement unit senses the motion of the pen body. The tip sits some distance from that sensor, so tilting the pen can move the tip even when the sensor barely translates. Separating those motions matters when reconstructing a stroke.

Research question

Could an optical measurement near the tip help distinguish motion across the page from drift or a pen lift?
