Class AB Audio Amplifier & Power Supply

I built a two-stage Class AB amplifier and a regulated 12 V AC-to-DC supply to drive a speaker. On the bench, I compared the output waveform with and without the speaker connected.

Testing under load

The waveform looked clean on the oscilloscope until I connected the speaker. Seeing it clip under load gave me a concrete question to investigate: how much current could the output stage deliver before the signal distorted?

Power supply

I built the linear regulated supply alongside the amplifier so I could test the complete signal path, from the audio input jack through the output stage to the speaker.
